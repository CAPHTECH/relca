# lim-standing.broken-overflow

ADR 0039; design v0.2 §22.3. Hand-derived before execution.

A control party may stand over the regular process end done; changing only that target to overflowed is unsupported, not a normal terminal Standing.
Single transformation: 'standing over [end.done]' -> 'standing over [end.overflowed]'.

P2 delay, P9 label and K mutations are not counted as single-input defect pairs.
