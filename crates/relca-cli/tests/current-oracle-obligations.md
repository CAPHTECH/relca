### R2: separate closed recovery raw population

ADR 0065 adds exactly fourteen named `r2-*` snapshot invocations. Their full
ordered argv, root exit/verdict and hand timelines are checked separately by
`support/r2_snapshot_oracle.rs`, using the declarations and interval sums in
`snapshots/inputs/r2-raw-notes.md`, not captured stdout. The classifier rejects
a missing job, an extra recovery job and changed argv. It checks the financial
evidence at3, decision/resolve at6, End/work7, the independent late-deadline
violation, clock/horizon distinction, unfinished work3, sampled vocabulary,
both comparison children, replay and explicit unsupported mutation/conformance.
Typed decoding supplements these values; it is not their semantic oracle.

These jobs must not enter the legacy graph walkers that deliberately reject
recovery IR. At R2 adoption all fourteen legacy CENSUS values, including 4271 Report slots,
3549 graph contexts and 77 modern-edit contexts, remained unchanged. ADR 0068 later
removes exactly two expiry mutants (current totals 4269 and 75); the graph count
and all other census categories remain unchanged. The new lane
counts and records all fourteen R2 jobs before removing just that new job count
from the legacy census comparison. The old six conform jobs retain their exact
set and checks; the new recovery conform refusal is checked in the R2 lane.
An unknown conform job still fails the old closed registry.

The raw delta audit separately recognizes the local R2 conclusion bundle on a
recovery owner or its comparison/sensitivity parent, and validates point child
ledgers as well. It does not allow arbitrary new bundle strings, normalize raw
stdout, or waive stderr/exit equality. Its original seven fault controls remain;
four additional hand bundle/ownership pairs reject the opposite bundle.

### U3: separate small independent-exploration lane

`completion_population_oracle.rs` and core `support/completion_oracle.rs` call
`engine::explore` for completeness and for their counterfactual queries. Their
existing names are retained. They derive completion inventory without the
production completion helper, but are **not independent of the explorer**: a
shared missing successor can affect both their expected and actual inventory.
The 3549 contexts / 3151 warning sites above are not claims of independent
transition exploration.

Core and CLI `completion_independent.rs` add **19 explicitly paired DSL /
handwritten OwnModel inputs**, not 19 more full-population contexts. The test-only
`support/completion_explorer.rs` has its own state, tasks, control, conditions,
dispatch, completion, clock/work, deduplication, deadlock/cut, inventories and
completion judgments. It imports no production engine, reference, completion,
report or agency helper. The separate data adapter reads IR fields only; full
handwritten-model equality and explicit type/end-table assertions check lowering
instead of using lower output as the source of expectations.

The lane covers single cases, capacity 0..2, finite enum results, named/anonymous
executors (including one person shared between Actors), small positive fixed
durations, sequential calls/if/end/wait, fork all, one independent resource and
clock cuts. Actual models use at most two live tasks. The adapter's bounded
supported fragment and its explicit `NotCovered` refusals are detailed in
`../../relca-core/tests/completion_independent_calculations.md`. Join any,
cancellation, requires, policies, multiple cases, kernels, atomic, contest and
quality are not silently delegated to the old oracle or counted as independent.

The comparison uses reachable ends, started capabilities, taken arms, assigned
people, deadlock/cut, terminal time/work sets, all four warning code/target/reason/
span payloads, completion/reachable properties and almost-sure probability
envelopes. Public Outcome does not expose Start/arm/person sets, so **only the
actual side** uses event-monitor/absorbing-end probes. Expected sets are generated
solely from OwnModel. The current hand population has singleton time/work sets
per end; non-singleton product extrema are not expanded into invented states.
No private State seam or internal product-state equality is claimed. Real CLI
JSON/root verdict/exit is also checked without snapshots. Comparator controls
reject missing successors, extra terminals, one-tick shifts, diagnostic edits
and missing/duplicate completion properties. The hand calculations and scope
above do not replace the existing whole-population counterfactual coverage.
Five one-at-a-time production engine faults (dropped fail successor, missing
capacity gate, concurrent reuse of a person, hidden clock cut, one-tick late
finish) each failed the new observation-set comparison once; each was restored
with zero production-src diff. This calibrates those five faults, not every
possible exploration fault.
